Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026 · Last updated: November 27, 2026
In short: Web Video Downloader is a web browser that helps you save videos from the pages you visit. It works on your device: there is no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and we do not run servers that receive your data. Your browsing data and download history stay on your phone and are not included in Android backups. The websites you open receive your requests directly, just as in any other browser, and handle your data under their own policies.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the Web Video Downloader Android app (“Web Video Downloader”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information. It applies to the app distributed on Google Play under the package name com.webvideodownloader.app. By using the app you agree to this Policy.

2. What the app does on your device

The app has a built-in browser. While you visit a web page, it detects the video files that page loads or plays, and lets you save the ones you choose to your phone.

  • No account required. You never sign in to the app or give us your name, email address or phone number.
  • Download history stays on the device. For each download the app keeps the address of the video and of the page it came from, its title, the file name and the progress, so that you can pause, resume, open or share it. This list is stored only in the app’s private storage.
  • Saved videos. Finished videos are saved to the public Download/Web Video Downloader folder on your device, where other apps such as your gallery or file manager can open them. Unfinished downloads are kept as temporary files in the app’s private storage.
  • Browser data. Cookies, cache and website storage created by the pages you visit are kept on your device by the Android System WebView component, as in other Android browsers.
  • Not backed up. The app opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so its download history and browser data are not copied to your Google account or to a new phone.

4. Websites you visit and the built-in browser

When you open a page or download a video, your device connects directly to the website concerned. Like any browser, the app sends that website the information needed to deliver its content, such as your IP address, the browser’s user agent (device and Android version), the address of the page and the cookies that website has set. Downloads carry the address of the page you were on and that same website’s cookies, so that they work in the same way as playing the video; cookies of one website are never sent to a different website. None of this passes through us.

Each website decides what it collects and how it uses it, under its own privacy policy. We do not control third party websites and are not responsible for their practices. The app does not support downloading from YouTube.

Android System WebView

Web pages are displayed by Android System WebView, a component of Android provided by Google. WebView uses Google Safe Browsing to warn you about dangerous sites, which involves checking web addresses against lists provided by Google. If you have allowed your device to send usage and diagnostics data to Google, WebView may also report crash and usage statistics under that setting. This processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not collect any personal information through the app. The app contains no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash reporting SDKs, and it does not send your browsing history, download history, files or device identifiers to us or to any partner of ours.

If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and the content of your message. We use it only to respond to you and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose.

6. Your choices

  • You can delete any download, and its file, from the app’s Downloads page.
  • To remove all download history and browser data (including cookies and website logins), clear the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → Web Video Downloader → Storage (the exact path may vary by device), or uninstall the app.
  • Videos you have saved to the Download folder remain on your device after uninstalling; delete them with your file manager if you no longer want them.
